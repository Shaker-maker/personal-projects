Questions

Frequently Asked

Everything you need to know before your visit.

How long does a full set take? ▼
A full acrylic set typically takes 1.5 – 2 hours. Gel polish appointments are about 1 hour.
Do you offer walk-ins? ▼
We recommend booking in advance to secure your preferred time. Walk-ins are welcome but subject to availability.
What payment methods do you accept? ▼
We accept M-Pesa, cash, and bank transfers. A deposit may be required for bridal packages.
Do you use premium products? ▼
Yes — we use CND, OPI, and other professional-grade brands. Your nail health is our priority.
How do I book? ▼
Simply tap the "Book on WhatsApp" button anywhere on this page and we'll get you scheduled.
